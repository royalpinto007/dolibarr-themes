# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

## Expected

- Take design disagreement seriously — say *why* something is wrong, ideally with a
  screenshot or a measurement rather than a preference
- Accept that reasonable people disagree about spacing, and that the tokens exist so
  those disagreements resolve in one place
- Help newcomers; the specificity traps in this codebase caught the author repeatedly
  and are documented in CONTRIBUTING.md for exactly that reason

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, publishing others' private
information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, commits and any project space.

## Enforcement

Report to <royalpinto007@gmail.com>. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may block repeat or
serious offenders.

Adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
kept short deliberately.
